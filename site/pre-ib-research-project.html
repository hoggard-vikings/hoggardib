About the project

All Pre-IB students complete a Pre-IB Research Project by the end of 10th grade (see Required Courses).

Research Questions will be presented at Hoggard’s Signature Program Night.

Pre-IB Research Project 2026-2027

Complete Year 2 Segment if continuing Topic OR Choose new topic and Complete Year 1 Segment

1. Music and Memory: How Songs Shape Generations
  • Year 1 Segment: Research how music trends from a chosen decade (e.g., 1980s, 2000s) reflected social/political events using music databases, newspaper archives, and books.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Conduct surveys/interviews with multiple generations (grandparents, parents, peers) about music’s impact on memory and identity, then compare findings to the research.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
2. Climate Change in Our Backyard
  • Year 1 Segment: Research historical climate data (temperature, rainfall, storm events) from databases and local archives. Compare to current conditions.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Create a field study by observing/documenting local environmental changes (tree blooming, bird migration, erosion, etc.) and compare findings to the historical record.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
3. Fashion as a Mirror of Culture
  • Year 1 Segment: Use fashion databases, old magazines, and library resources to research fashion in a specific decade and how it reflected cultural values.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Collect oral histories or interviews from family/community members about their clothing choices and what they meant socially/politically. Compare lived experiences to research.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
4. Sports and Society: Beyond the Game
  • Year 1 Segment: Research the evolution of a sport (e.g., soccer, basketball, skateboarding) using sports archives and scholarly databases. Focus on how rules, access, or popularity changed.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Interview local athletes/coaches, or analyze sports coverage in school newspapers and yearbooks over time. Compare how local context reflects broader trends.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
5. Food and Identity: The Stories We Eat
  • Year 1 Segment: Research the history of a particular dish or cuisine (e.g., tacos, sushi, pizza) using food history databases and cookbooks. Connect to migration, trade, or cultural exchange.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Collect recipes, oral histories, and cooking traditions from local families. Compare community food stories with the academic history.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
6. Technology Then and Now
  • Year 1 Segment: Research the invention, adoption, and societal impact of a chosen technology (e.g., phones, cars, video games) using patents, library archives, and databases.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Conduct interviews with different generations on how they used or experienced that technology. Compare perceptions with historical data.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
7. Hidden Histories of Our Community
  • Year 1 Segment: Use local archives, newspapers, and historical society records to uncover a “forgotten” story from the community (e.g., a landmark, event, or person- I am going to exclude the Wilmington Coup since you have learned about this quite a bit in school, but another “forgotten” Wilmington story would work for this!).
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Gather oral histories, photographs, or artifacts from community members to expand on the narrative. Present a comparison of documented vs. lived history.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
8. Language Across Time
  • Year 1 Segment: Research how slang or a particular word/phrase has changed over time using linguistic databases, old dictionaries, and newspapers.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Create a living language study by surveying peers, teachers, and family members about word usage today. Compare with archival findings.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
9. The Science of Happiness
  • Year 1 Segment: Research how psychologists and scientists have studied happiness (using psychology databases, scholarly journals, and non-digital library resources).
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Design and conduct your own small-scale study or survey on happiness factors among peers, then compare results to established research.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
10. Art and Social Movements
  • Year 1 Segment: Research a period of art that was tied to social or political change (Harlem Renaissance, street art, feminist art, etc.) using art history databases and books.
    • Find sources from Academic Databases
    • Develop a Research Question
  • Year 2 Segment: Collect primary sources (interviews with local artists, visits to galleries/museums, photographs of public art) to connect local creative expression with the historical movement.
    • Use AI to brainstorm ways that you can complete the Year 2 Segment of the Research Project
    • Develop a Research Question and attempt an answer
    • Create Research Presentation for the Community
