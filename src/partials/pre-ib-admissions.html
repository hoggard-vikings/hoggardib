Pre-IB Admissions and Enrollment:

-The Enrollment period begins for incoming 9th-11th graders in December and ends in January-Pre-IB means fulfilling course prerequisites for IB classes, participating in IB Flex and Mentoring Programs, and participating in Creativity, Activity, and Service through Hoggard Clubs. Please note that the pre-IB programme is not part of or affiliated with the IB programmes. This class/programme is not a requirement to enter the 2-year IB Diploma programme or the IB Career-related programme
