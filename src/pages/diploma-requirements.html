The Diploma Programme

The IB Diploma Programme is a two-year experience for 11th and 12th graders that prepares them for college and an increasingly global world. Students take a balanced set of demanding courses plus three core components that build critical thinking, research skills and personal growth. There are 3 pathways: Diploma, Certificate, or Course.

To earn the IB Diploma, students:

  • Take six IB courses across subject areas (English, a world language, math, science, history, and an arts or elective option), with 3 to 4 at Higher Level (HL) and the rest at Standard Level (SL).
  • Complete the IB Core: Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay and CAS.
  • Take IB exams at the end, which include written assessments, projects, and sometimes oral presentations or performances.
  • Maintain academic integrity, commitment and steady engagement for both years.

To earn the IB Certificate, students:

  • Take at least three IB courses over 2 years
  • Take part in the IB Core: CAS and TOK as an optional class
  • Take the end-of-programme IB exams.
  • Maintain academic integrity, commitment and engagement.

To be an IB Course Student, students:

  • Take 1-2 IB classes without participating in the IB Core Curriculum
