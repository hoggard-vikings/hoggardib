IB Diploma pathway: required courses

Pre-IB Course Requirements for 9th-10th Graders

9th grade (must haves)

  • Hon. English
  • Hon. Civ. Lit or AP Government
  • Spanish or French I-II

10th grade (must haves)

  • Hon. English 2
  • AP World (or Honors World)
  • Spanish III-IV (recommended for Spanish SL and required for Spanish HL) or French III

By the end of 10th grade they should have the following courses completed:

  • Hon. Eng 1 and 2
  • Hon. Bio and Hon. Chem
    (APES is recommended but sometimes students take this during their 11th or 12th grade years)
  • Hon Civ (or AP Government) and AP World (or Hon World)
  • Hon. Math 1, Hon Math 2, Hon. Math 3
    (Those planning to take IB Math Analysis SL will need to take Pre-Calc; Those planning to take IB Math Analysis HL will need to take Calc)
  • Health/PE
  • World Language-at least three levels of the following choices: Spanish or French
    (IB HL Spanish requires four levels Spanish and IB SL Spanish and French recommends four levels of French)

In addition, all Pre-IB students complete by the end of 10th grade:

  • 15 hours of community service (5 hours in 9th grade, 10 hours in 10th grade). See Service.
  • A Pre-IB Research Project.
  • Participation in IB Enrichment.

IB Certificate pathway: required courses

The Certificate pathway is for students taking 2 to 4 IB classes. On top of electives and NC graduation requirements, students complete these by the end of 10th grade:

  • Honors English 1 and 2.
  • Spanish or French 1 and 2. This is the minimum; students who like languages are encouraged to go further.
  • Honors Civics & Literacy or AP Government.
  • Honors World History or AP World History.
  • Biology and Chemistry, on-level or honors.
  • Math 1, 2 and 3, on-level or honors.
  • Health and PE.
  • Participation in community service and enrichment.
