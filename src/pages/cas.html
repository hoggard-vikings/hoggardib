Creativity, Activity, Service

CAS is a journey of self-discovery in which students participate in a range of activities to meet seven learning outcomes.

CAS at Hoggard

  • Students do experiences in creativity, activity and service over 18 months, starting in the first semester of junior year and running through March of senior year.
  • They also do a month-long collaborative project that combines creativity, activity, service and teamwork.
  • CAS is a core component of the Diploma Programme, grounded in reflection and local and global outreach.
