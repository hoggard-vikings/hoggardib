What is the IB Diploma?

  1. i.It is a pre-university diploma and international admissions credential.
  2. ii.It utilizes international standards.
  3. iii.It is based on an integrated curriculum.
  4. iv.It is infused with international mindedness within and across the curriculum creating more global awareness for the school in and out of the classroom.
  5. v.It is present in public and private schools, magnet schools, Comprehensive high schools, International schools, and schools looking to redesign.
